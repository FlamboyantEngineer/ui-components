`SegmentedChoice` takes `iconOnly="auto"`: with an icon on every option, the segments fold to icons, named by their labels and shown in tooltips, while the labels do not fit the width the parent gives the group, and show their labels again once it widens. `ListFilter` options take an `icon`, and `ListFilter` takes `iconOnly`, `auto` by default, so filters with icons fold to icons in a narrow row.
