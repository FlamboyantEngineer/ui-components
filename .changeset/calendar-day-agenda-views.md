`CalendarView` adds a day view (the week's time grid for the focused day, moving by a day) and an agenda view (the focused day's week listed by day under headings, with `agendaEmptyDays` and `renderEmptyState`). Every view is allowed by default, and the provider strings gain `day`, `agenda` and `noEvents`, with `previousRange` and `nextRange` naming a day.
