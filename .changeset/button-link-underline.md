Button gains `underline` (`none` by default, `hover` or `always`) for the `link` variant, published as `data-underline`; a link button is no longer underlined on hover and focus unless asked. The reference sheet drops the native underline of buttons drawn as links and sizes button icons with the button: 12, 14, 16 and 20 pixels from `xs` to `lg` (#1420).
