Add a board and a calendar view.

- `Board` (new): columns of cards from plain data (`columns` with `title` and `limit`, `items`, `getColumn`, `getTextValue`, `renderCard`), optional collapsible `lanes` (`getLane`), column headers with counts against limits (`data-over-limit`), collapsible columns and lanes (`collapsedColumns`/`collapsedLanes`, controlled or uncontrolled, remembered with `persistKey`), empty cells, an add-card button per cell (`onAddCard`), card activation (`onAction`) and `virtualized` cells. Each cell is a React Aria grid list: with `onMove`, cards move within and between cells by pointer and keyboard drag and drop and Alt+Arrow keys, reported as `{ key, column, lane, targetKey, position }`; with `onColumnMove`, columns reorder by dragging their header, from their handle by keyboard, or with Alt+ArrowLeft/Right. `applyBoardMove` and `applyColumnMove` apply moves to the owner's arrays. Hooks: `ui-board` and its `__columns`, `__column`, `__column-handle`, `__column-toggle`, `__column-title`, `__column-count`, `__lane`, `__lane-header`, `__lane-toggle`, `__lane-title`, `__lane-count`, `__cells`, `__cell`, `__cards`, `__card`, `__card-handle`, `__card-content`, `__drop-indicator`, `__empty` and `__add` slots, with `data-collapsed`, `data-over-limit`, `data-virtualized` and the drag and drop states.
- `CalendarView` (new): events on ISO 8601 dates (all-day) and date-times (timed) in a month or week view (`view`, `views`), placed in `timeZone`; multi-day events as bars across days packed into lanes, crowded month days behind a `+n more` popover (`maxEvents`), a week time grid placed with `createTimeScale`, today from `getNow`, a toolbar (Today, previous, next, view choice), keyboard navigation between days (`date`, `onDateChange`), `onEventAction` and a `renderDay` slot. Hooks: `ui-calendar-view` with `data-view` and its toolbar, grid, day and event slots, plus `data-today`, `data-outside-month`, `data-date`, `data-all-day`, `data-tone` and `data-continues-before`/`-after`.
- Provider strings: `addCard`, `noCards`, `cardCount`, `moveColumn`, `month`, `week`, `calendarViews`, `previousRange`, `nextRange`, `allDay`, `moreEvents` and `calendarEvent`, in a new planning strings module.
