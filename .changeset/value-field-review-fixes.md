Value field fixes. `errorSources` is no longer a prop of field controls; it stays an option of `useFieldState` and `useFieldControl`. A natively validated `Form` focuses the first blocking field after a blocked submit, a period, date or colour field included. `DecimalField` reads back the negative values right-to-left locales write, and its scale message says "steps of 1" for `scale={0}`. The colour picker keeps a grey's hue and black's saturation while dragging, and a hex value rounds fractional RGB channels. A plain `NumberField` submits with the form named by `form`. A read-only slider marks its thumbs `aria-readonly`. `PeriodField` names Gregorian months in locales whose calendar has other months, and picking the chosen period closes the grid. `MultiDateField` drops a text that is no date from its tags, form entries and next change, and a form focuses its calendar day rather than its month buttons. A floating date and time in a daylight saving gap keeps its digits when formatted. Field value equality handles invalid dates and big integers and compares maps, sets and files by identity. The form layout designer mirrors row and grid drops right to left and reads a repeated announcement again.
