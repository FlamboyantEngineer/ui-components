The reference stylesheet keeps horizontal `Steps` labels readable in narrow containers. Steps share the width in equal columns with their labels under the markers; below 80px a column, and always in the compact variant, the markers form a rail and the current step's text takes a full-width line below, so 4 to 8 steps fit from 240px without letter-by-letter wrapping or overlapping parts.
