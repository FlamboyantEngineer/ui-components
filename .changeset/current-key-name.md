The current item has one name, `currentKey`: `Wizard` (`currentKey`, `defaultCurrentKey`, `onCurrentKeyChange`), `Outline`, `Steps`, `StateMachineView` (was `currentState`), `AnnotationLayer` (was `activeId`, `defaultActiveId` and `onActiveChange`) and `Tree`, whose `currentKey` replaces the `isItemCurrent` callback. `ChatList` takes `selectedKey` instead of `selectedId`.
