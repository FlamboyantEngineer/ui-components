StateMachineView sequences now carry a typed host contract. `onStateAction` and `onTransitionAction` fire on every press, Enter or Space with the key, the definition and the press event, apart from selection. `selectedTransitionKey` with `onTransitionSelectionChange` replaces `activeTransitionKey` (the route and label hook is now `data-selected`), and `onSelectionChange` may receive `null` when a selected state or transition is removed or disabled. States form one roving tab stop with arrow, Home and End keys, take `isDisabled`, and keep focus on a neighbour when the focused state goes away. `onStateContextMenu`, `onTransitionContextMenu` (now with the transition and a request event) and `onBackgroundContextMenu` receive pointer, Context Menu key and Shift+F10 requests with a menu anchor. States take `status`, `tone`, `description`, a structural `count` or a capped `notificationCount` with `countLabel`, `renderStateAdornment` and `getStateTooltip` with `stateTooltipPlacement`; transitions take `description`. An empty sequence shows a focusable note set by `renderEmptyState` and `emptyLabel`. `ContextMenu` also exports the `MenuAnchor` and `MenuPoint` types.
