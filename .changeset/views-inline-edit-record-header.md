Add `ViewSwitcher`, `DisplayOptions`, `InlineEdit`, `RecordHeader` and `TransitionMenu`.

- `ViewSwitcher`: saved views of a collection (`views`: id, label, icon) as tabs with the selected view's content in a panel, or in a menu (`variant`: `tabs`, `menu`); `selectedKey`, `defaultSelectedKey`, `onSelectionChange`; a modified mark while `isDirty`; a default view marked with a star (`defaultViewKey`, `onDefaultViewChange`); an actions menu and per-tab menu with save (`onSave`), save as a new named view (`onSaveAs`), reset (`onReset`), rename (`onRename`) and delete after a confirmation (`onDelete`). Hooks: `ui-view-switcher` and its slots, `data-variant`, `data-dirty`.
- `DisplayOptions`: a button and popover choosing how a collection shows its rows as plain data (`value`, `defaultValue`, `onChange`: `{ groupBy, sort, properties, density }`): group by and sort by among `properties` that allow it (`allowsGrouping`, `allowsSorting`), the sort direction, the shown properties with a checkbox each and their order by drag and drop, and the row spacing among `densities` (`compact`, `default`, `comfortable`); `label`, `isDisabled`. Hooks: `ui-display-options` and its slots, `data-shown`, `data-disabled`.
- `InlineEdit`: a value read in place (`value`, `renderValue`, `placeholder`) that turns into its editor on a press or F2 (`renderEditor` with `value`, `onChange`, `commit`, `cancel`, `label`, `isPending`; a text field by default), saves on Enter or leaving and cancels on Escape, with an asynchronous `onSave` that stays pending and shows a failure; `showActions`, `isEditing`, `defaultEditing`, `onEditingChange`, `isDisabled`. Hooks: `ui-inline-edit` and its slots, `data-editing`, `data-pending`, `data-invalid`, `data-empty`, `data-disabled`.
- `RecordHeader`: a record's title row: kind icon and identifier with a copy button, a title heading editable in place (`onTitleChange`, `titlePlaceholder`, `headingLevel`), and `status`, `meta` (a meta line) and `actions` slots. Hooks: `ui-record-header` and its slots, `data-editable`.
- `TransitionMenu`: the current state (`states`, `value`) as a badge opening the allowed `transitions` (target tone, description, `disabledReason`), each run through `onTransition`, optionally after a confirmation (`confirmMessage`) or a required comment (`requiresComment`); `label`, `isDisabled`. Hooks: `ui-transition-menu` and its slots, `data-target-tone`, `data-pending`, `data-disabled`, `data-readonly`.
- New glyphs: `star`, `adjustments`.
- New provider strings (in a new view strings module): `views`, `viewActions`, `viewModified`, `defaultView`, `saveViewAs`, `resetView`, `setDefaultView`, `newView`, `renameView`, `viewName`, `deleteViewTitle`, `deleteViewMessage`, `display`, `displayOptions`, `groupBy`, `noGrouping`, `sortBy`, `noSorting`, `sortDirection`, `sortDirectionName`, `shownProperties`, `showProperty`, `density`, `densityName`, `editValue`, `saveFailed`, `recordTitle`, `copyIdentifier`, `status`, `stateTrigger`, `transitionTo`.
