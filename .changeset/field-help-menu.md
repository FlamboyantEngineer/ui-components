A field with `help` offers Help in its context menu (added once, also to custom and empty menus), and choosing it shows the help in a tooltip against the help button or the control. `showHelpButton={false}` hides the question-mark button: the help then describes the control and stays reachable from the menu. Every field control inherits both.
