Add `ColorSwatchButton`, an icon button whose glyph sits over a bar of the colour it applies, described by that colour's name, for text and fill colour commands. Add four glyphs, each overridable through `UiProvider`: `font-color` (a capital A) and `background-color` (a paint bucket), which leave room for that bar, and `increase-font-size` and `decrease-font-size`. `NumberField` takes `stepValues`, preset values such as font sizes that its step buttons and arrow keys move through while any number can still be typed, and `decrementIcon` and `incrementIcon` to replace its step buttons' glyphs.
