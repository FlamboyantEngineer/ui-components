`shadcn.css` draws navigation and structure like shadcn/ui's "nova" components: tabs as a 32 pixel muted list of 25 pixel tabs (the line variant marking the selected tab at the list's foot) over an unpadded panel; breadcrumbs as muted links beside 14 pixel chevrons; pagination as 32 pixel ghost buttons with the current page outlined; cards with 16 pixel (12 when small) spacing and a full-padded footer band; accordions divided by hairlines with end chevrons and underlined titles; banners as card-colored boxes whatever their tone, danger in the destructive color; shortcut keycaps as muted chips; skeletons on the muted fill; progress bars and meters on a 4 pixel track; avatars with muted initials and a hairline ring; and empty states with the glyph in a muted square.
