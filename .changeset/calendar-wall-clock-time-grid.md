Calendar View places week-view events on the same 24-hour wall-clock ruler as its hour labels, so on days when the clocks change an event sits under its own time and level with the same time on other days. The skipped hour stays empty, both passes of a repeated hour share its place side by side, and event times in a repeated hour name their offsets.
