`Tree` groups its top-level items into category sections with `getSection`: each section is a row group named by its own heading row, which takes no focus, selection or depth, while arrows, Home, End, typeahead, filtering, selection and virtualization run across the sections. Adds the `TreeSectionData` type and the `ui-tree__section` and `ui-tree__section-header` hooks.
