Add a guided tour, an avatar group, a carousel and a QR code with its encoder.

- `Tour` (new): a guided sequence of `steps` (`{ target, title, content, placement }`, the target a ref or a CSS selector) shown one at a time in a dialog floating against the target, with a decorative spotlight over it, the position (`2 of 5`), Skip, Back and Next or Finish, Escape to skip, focus moved to each step and returned on close; modal by default (the page inert) or `isNonModal`; `step`/`defaultStep`/`onStepChange`, the open state props, `onFinish`, `onSkip`, and `persistKey`, which remembers completion through the provider storage. Hooks: `ui-tour` with `__arrow`, `__dialog`, `__header`, `__title`, `__progress`, `__body`, `__footer`, `__skip`, `__back`, `__next` and `__spotlight`, plus `data-non-modal` and `data-target-missing`.
- `AvatarGroup` (new): an overlapping stack of `Avatar`s for `people` with a `+N` count past `maxVisible`, named by everyone's names, `size`, `variant`, `colorCount`, and `action="list"`, which makes the stack a button opening a popover that lists everyone with a `description`. Hooks: `ui-avatar-group` with `__avatar`, `__overflow`, `__trigger`, `__popover`, `__list` and `__item`, plus `data-size` and `data-action`.
- `Carousel` (new): slides (one per child) shown one at a time on the ARIA carousel pattern, with previous and next buttons, dots or thumbnails (`pagination`, `renderThumbnail`), `loop`, the arrow keys, focus kept on a slide that hides, `index`/`defaultIndex`/`onIndexChange`, and `autoplay` with `interval`, pausing on hover and focus, starting stopped for reduced motion, with a rotation button and the live region off while rotating. Hooks: `ui-carousel` with `__controls`, `__rotation`, `__previous`, `__next`, `__viewport`, `__slide`, `__pagination`, `__dot` and `__thumbnail`, plus `data-pagination`, `data-rotating` and `data-current`.
- `QrCode` (new): text drawn as an SVG QR code with an `errorCorrection` level, a `quietZone` and a `moduleSize`, named by the text it holds. Hooks: `ui-qr-code` with `__modules`, plus `data-error-correction`, `data-version` and `data-invalid`.
- `encodeQr` (new utility): a pure QR encoder (UTF-8 byte mode, versions 1 to 40, the four error correction levels, Reed–Solomon codewords, interleaving and the least penalised mask) returning `{ version, errorCorrection, mask, size, modules }`, or `undefined` for text too long.
- Glyphs: `play` and `pause`. Provider strings: `carousel`, `slide`, `previousSlide`, `nextSlide`, `chooseSlide`, `goToSlide`, `startSlideRotation`, `stopSlideRotation`, `peopleCount`, `qrCodeOf` and `qrCodeTooLong`, in a new presentation strings module.
