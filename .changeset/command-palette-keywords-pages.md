CommandPalette: commands given as `items` can carry search `keywords` and rank their matches (whole label, label start, word start, anywhere, then keywords), and a command with `commands` opens a page in place, with a Back button, Escape and Backspace going back, and `path`, `defaultPath` and `onPathChange`. New types `CommandPaletteEntry`, `CommandPaletteItem` and `CommandPaletteSection`.
