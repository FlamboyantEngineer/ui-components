Draw `FourSidePropertyStrip` links as brackets below the linked fields. With `link`, the row publishes `data-link` and draws one `ui-four-side-property-strip__bracket` per group of sides that link together (`data-sides`): all four, or each opposite pair. The link toggle sits at the centre of the first bracket, and a later bracket shows a decorative `ui-four-side-property-strip__glyph`. The reference sheet draws each bracket as `|----icon----|`, with its ends under the group's outer controls.
