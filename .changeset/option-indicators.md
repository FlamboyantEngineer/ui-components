A single select's chosen option and a combobox's selected options draw a decorative check after their content (`ui-select-field__option-indicator`, data-slot `select-item-indicator`; `ui-combobox__option-indicator`, data-slot `combobox-item-indicator`), so a sheet can mark the choice as shadcn/ui does. The reference sheet hides it.
