Add comment threads and reactions.

- `CommentThread` (new): a discussion with a quoted anchor (`quote`), the opening comment and its replies as children, resolve and reopen (`isResolved`, `defaultResolved`, `onResolvedChange`), `isReadOnly`, `isSelected`, `visibleReplies` (long threads hide their middle replies behind "Show N more replies"), an `error`, and a composer slot: `onReply` draws the default `CommentComposer`, `composer` replaces it. Hooks: `ui-comment-thread` with `__header`, `__quote`, `__status`, `__resolve`, `__comments`, `__item`, `__more`, `__show-more`, `__error`, `__composer`, `data-resolved`, `data-readonly`, `data-selected`.
- `CommentThreadReply` (new): one comment with its `author`, a live `time`, `isEdited`, the body as caller content (`children`, else `text`), a `reactions` slot, and a menu of caller `actions` (`onAction`) plus Edit in place (`onEdit`, `renderEditor`) and Delete after confirmation (`onDelete`). Hooks: `ui-comment-thread-reply` with `__header`, `__author`, `__time`, `__edited`, `__actions`, `__body`, `__editor`, `__reactions`, `data-edited`, `data-editing`.
- `CommentComposer` (new): a multi-line `TextField` with submit and cancel; Ctrl+Enter or Command+Enter submits, Escape cancels, a promise from `onSubmit` keeps it pending and keeps the draft on failure, and `storageKey` keeps the draft in the provider storage. Hooks: `ui-comment-composer` with `__field`, `__actions`, `__cancel`, `__submit`, `data-pending`, `data-disabled`.
- `ReactionBar` (new): reaction chips (emoji, count, pressed while the reader reacted) named by who reacted (`maxNames`), toggled through `onToggle`, and an add-reaction button that opens the `EmojiPicker` (`customEmoji`, `recentEmoji`, `onRecentEmojiChange`, `loadEmoji`); read-only without `onToggle`. Hooks: `ui-reaction-bar` with `__reaction`, `__emoji`, `__image`, `__count`, `__add`, `data-readonly`.
- New glyph `emoji` and strings `commentThread`, `commentActions`, `addComment`, `comment`, `reply`, `editComment`, `edited`, `resolve`, `reopen`, `resolved`, `showMoreReplies`, `deleteComment`, `deleteCommentMessage`, `reactions`, `addReaction` and `reactionLabel`.
