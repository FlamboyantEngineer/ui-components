Fields take `changeLayout` (`inline` by default, or `stacked`), published as `data-change-layout`, to choose where a dirty field's control shows the value its edit replaced: before the current value on its row, or on its own line above it. In the reference sheet, an inline SelectField previous value keeps its width up to half the row. The SelectField lab shows changed, filled, emptied, long and multiple edits in both layouts and at narrow widths.
