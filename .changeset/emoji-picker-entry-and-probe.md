EmojiPicker: Enter in the search chooses nothing while the standard emoji load or after they failed to, since no emoji, custom ones included, is shown. Down from the search focuses the first emoji through the grid's collection, so a virtualized grid scrolled past it brings it into view. A canvas that cannot measure, such as one whose readback the browser denies, counts as unknown support instead of crashing the picker, and the failure is not kept.
