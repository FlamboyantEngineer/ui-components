`DateTimeField` takes `firstDayOfWeek` to start the calendar's weeks on a chosen day, and `weekNumbers` (`iso` or `locale`) to show each week's number as its row header (`ui-date-time-field__calendar-week`, under `ui-date-time-field__calendar-week-label`). ISO numbers start the weeks on Monday unless `firstDayOfWeek` says otherwise. Adds the `weekColumn` and `weekNumber` strings.
