NumberField's double-arrow scrub handle also steps the value with the wheel, and `endAdornmentPlacement` puts the end adornment (a unit) at the frame's edge or right after the number, with the `ui-number-field__value` and `ui-number-field__sizer` hooks and `data-adornment-placement`. The reference sheet scales the step and scrub glyphs with the field's size.
