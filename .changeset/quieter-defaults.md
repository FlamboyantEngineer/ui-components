Quieter defaults. `NumberField` and `DecimalField` draw their step buttons only with `showStepper` (it replaces `hideStepper`; the root publishes `data-show-stepper`). Shortcuts draw as quiet text by default; `appearance="keycaps"` or `ShortcutAppearanceContext` set to `keycaps` boxes the keys. `FormulaBar` no longer shows an evaluated result beside its content: `evaluate`, the `ui-formula-bar__result` slot and the `formulaResult` string are removed.

The reference sheet's warning tone is a gold: `--ref-warning` is a yellower ink that stays readable as text, and the new `--ref-warning-fill` and `--ref-on-warning-fill` are the bright yellow that rating stars, highlights, annotation marks and over-limit board headers take, and every tone fill, wash and frame takes the new `--ref-tone-fill` (with `--ref-on-tone-fill` for text on it), which is that gold fill, under dark text, for the warning tone. A menubar draws like a toolbar, as wide as its container with no border or background; Transfer List frames only its two lists; Approval Panel sits on the canvas without lines between approvers; a selected state in State Machine View takes a pale wash instead of a ring; an over-limit board column marks only its header; annotation marks are a pale yellow highlight without an underline; and custom rating glyphs no longer draw the empty glyph under the filled one.
