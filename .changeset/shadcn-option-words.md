shadcn/ui option words: `Button` and `Badge` take `variant="outline"` (bordered, no fill); `SegmentedChoice` takes `variant` (`default`, `outline`, segments with their own borders); `ListRow` takes `variant` (`default`, `outline`, `muted`) and `size` (`xs`, `sm`, `md`); `TabsList` takes `variant` (`default`, `line`); `Card` takes `size` (`sm`, `md`). Each publishes `data-variant` or `data-size` and is drawn in the reference and shadcn/ui stylesheets.
