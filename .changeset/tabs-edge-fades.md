A horizontal `TabList` that scrolls draws a decorative `ui-tabs__fade` over each edge that hides tabs, as a toolbar scroller does, inside a `ui-tabs__bar`. `showEdgeFades={false}` leaves them out. The reference stylesheet lets the list glide (`scroll-behavior: smooth`) as the wheel, a scroll button or a selection moves it.
