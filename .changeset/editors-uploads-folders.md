`CodeEditor` adds editing keys: Enter keeps the indentation (one level more after an opening bracket), `indentWithTab` and `indent` make Tab and Shift+Tab indent and outdent (Escape then Tab leaves), and `autoCloseBrackets` pairs brackets and quotes; the bracket at the caret and its partner are marked (`ui-code-editor__bracket`, `data-unmatched`). `completions` also takes a source (`CodeCompletionSource`, sync or async, with `from`/`to` ranges), Ctrl+Space asks for completions, matching ranks prefixes first and then scattered matches from word starts, and completions take `kind` (`data-kind`), `documentation` (`ui-code-editor__completion-documentation`) and snippet `insertText` with `${…}` fields stepped through with Tab. Diagnostics take `endLine`/`endColumn` and are underlined in the source (`ui-code-editor__mark`), F8 and Shift+F8 move between them and read them out (`ui-code-editor__announcement`), a hover tip shows them with the caller's `renderHover` content (`ui-code-editor__tooltip`), and `listDiagnostics={false}` keeps the marks without the list. `allowsSearch` adds a find and replace row on Ctrl+F (`ui-code-editor__search`, `__search-input`, `__replace-input`, `__search-status`, `ui-code-editor__match` with `data-current`).

`JsonEditor` marks where the text stops parsing in the editor, takes a `schema` (a documented JSON Schema subset, `JsonSchema`) that it checks, completes keys and values from and documents on hover, and passes `autoCloseBrackets` and `indent` to its editor.

`FileUpload` adds `maxFiles`, `minSize` and an owner check `validate` (sync or async) with the refusal reasons `count`, `min-size` and `invalid`; drag feedback on the drop zone (`data-drop-accept`, `data-drop-reject`); image thumbnails for listed files with a `file` or `previewUrl` (`ui-file-upload__file-preview`); folders with `acceptDirectory`; and forms with `name`, `isRequired`, `isInvalid` and `errorMessage` (`ui-file-upload__error`, `data-required`, `data-invalid`).

`FolderManager` loads subfolders on demand (`hasChildren`, `onLoadChildren`, `ui-folder-manager__load-error`), selects several folders with `selectionMode="multiple"` (`selectedKeys`, `defaultSelectedKeys`, `onSelectedKeysChange`) to delete them together or move them with the context menu's Move to, filters folders with `filterable` (`ui-folder-manager__filter`), and takes `virtualized` and `rowHeight`. `Tree` adds `virtualized` and `rowHeight` (`data-virtualized`).

Behaviour to note: CodeEditor completion lists now also show scattered matches after the prefix matches, Shift+Tab no longer inserts the active completion, and Enter keeps the line's indentation; `FileRejectionReason` has new members.
