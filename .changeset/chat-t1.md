`ChatTranscript` behaviour for chats. The transcript is now a `feed` of message articles (no longer a `log` live region) and one tab stop: Up, Down, Home and End move between messages, Enter moves into a message's controls (Tab cycles them; they stay out of the tab order otherwise), Escape returns to the message and a second Escape calls `onEscape`, and Page Up and Page Down scroll natively, then focus the message at the top. New messages are announced through its own polite live region, grouped and at most once a second, skipping the reader's own and announcing a streamed reply once it completes. New props: `scrollMode` (`follow`, or `anchor` to keep the reader's sent message at the top while the reply fills in, with a `ui-chat-transcript__spacer`), `onLoadOlder`, `hasOlder` and `isLoadingOlder` (older history loads near the top while the reader keeps their place, with a `ui-chat-transcript__history` indicator), `highlightedMessageId` (scrolls to, focuses and marks a message with `data-highlighted`), `firstUnreadId` (a `ui-chat-transcript__unread` bar reading "12 unread" with a Jump button), `onRead` (the newest message seen) and `virtualized` (only messages near the view are mounted). The jump button counts what arrived while the reader was away ("3 new messages"). Messages are found by their `id`, else their React key. New hooks: `ui-chat-transcript__feed`, `__history`, `__spacer`, `__unread`, `__announcer`, `data-virtualized` and `data-highlighted`; new strings in `ChatStrings`.
