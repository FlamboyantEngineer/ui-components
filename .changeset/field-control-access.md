`TextField` takes `inputRef` and `textAreaRef` for its input or text area, and `controlProps` for the control's own text, selection and composition events and the ARIA of a suggestion list the caller owns; the field's own handlers and completion attributes keep running first. `SelectField` takes `triggerRef` for its trigger button in list and tree mode.
