`SegmentedBar` takes `xl`, `2xl` and `3xl` sizes in every shape. `SegmentedBarSize` is now the bar's own type, so the shared `Size` scale and the other components are unchanged. The reference sheet draws 16, 20 and 24 pixel tracks and 48, 64 and 96 pixel rings at those sizes, with a larger label, total and legend.
