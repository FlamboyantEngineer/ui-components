`ColorField` takes `hueControl`: `wheel` edits the hue on a React Aria colour wheel (`ui-color-field__wheel`, `ui-color-field__wheel-track`) in place of the hue slider, keeping saturation, brightness and alpha. The dialog publishes `data-hue-control`. Exports the `ColorFieldHueControl` type.
