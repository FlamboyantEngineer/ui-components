Card's header row now holds the grid item's selection checkbox, the leading content, the title, the expand and collapse control and More, in that order, with the actions on their own line after them, so header controls no longer overlap each other or the title. A `CardGridItem` hands its selection checkbox to the card it holds, which draws it first in its header row.
