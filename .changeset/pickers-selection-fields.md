Add pickers and selection fields: a grid size picker, a character grid, a transfer list and a tree select field.

- `GridSizePicker` (new): chooses `{ rows, columns }` from a grid of cells (`onSelect`), previewing the hovered or focused size in a `3 × 4` readout and growing one row and column past the preview from `minRows`/`minColumns` (5) to `maxRows`/`maxColumns` (10); arrow keys (mirrored right to left), Home, End and Ctrl or Cmd with them move the preview, Enter or Space chooses; `isDisabled`, `autoFocus`. Hooks: `ui-grid-size-picker` with `__grid`, `__row`, `__cell` and `__readout`, plus `data-in-range`, `data-active` and `data-disabled`.
- `CharacterGrid` (new): a searchable two-dimensional grid of the caller's `categories` of `{ char, name }`, one React Aria grid list headed by category, with search by name, character or code point, a preview with the code points, and the recently chosen characters first, kept through the provider's storage under `persistKey`; `onSelect`, `autoFocus`. Hooks: `ui-character-grid` with `__search`, `__grid`, `__section`, `__heading`, `__cell`, `__message`, `__preview`, `__preview-char`, `__preview-name` and `__preview-code`, plus `data-searching`.
- `TransferList` (new): available and chosen `ListView`s over `items` (`value`/`defaultValue`/`onChange` hold the chosen ids in order), each with a filter, counts and multiple selection; buttons move the selection or every shown item, a double click or Enter moves a row, `disabledKeys` and `isDisabled` lock items, and `reorderable` reorders the chosen side by drag and drop and with move up and down buttons; `availableLabel`, `chosenLabel`. Hooks: `ui-transfer-list` with `__pane`, `__header`, `__title`, `__count`, `__search`, `__list`, `__empty`, `__actions`, `__move` and `__reorder`, plus `data-side`, `data-scope`, `data-target`, `data-reorderable` and `data-disabled`.
- `TreeSelectField` (new): a field (on the `Field` base) that chooses one node or several (`selectionMode`) from a hierarchy in a popover with a search (matches keep their ancestors) and a `Tree` (`getChildren`, `hasChildItems`, `onLoadChildren` for lazy branches), cascading checkboxes for multiple choice (`selectionPropagation`, on by default) with one removable chip per chosen branch, the chosen node read as its label or path (`valueDisplay`), `placeholder`, `isClearable`, `name` and the open state props. Hooks: `ui-tree-select-field` with `__frame`, `__trigger`, `__value`, `__chevron`, `__clear`, `__popover`, `__dialog`, `__search`, `__tree`, `__chips` and `__chip`, plus `data-selection-mode`, `data-value-display`, `data-placeholder` and `data-missing`.
- Provider strings: `gridSizePicker`, `gridSize`, `gridSizeCell`, `characterGrid`, `searchCharacters`, `recentCharacters`, `availableItems`, `chosenItems`, `noItems`, `moveSelectedTo` and `moveAllTo`, in a new picker strings module.
