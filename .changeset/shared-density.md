Densities share one core `Density` type (`tight`, `compact`, `default`, `comfortable`) with `default` as the baseline. `ButtonGroup` and `ToolbarRowGroup` density `regular` is now `default`; `Steps` takes `density` (`default` or `compact`, published as `data-density`) instead of `variant`; `CardDensity` is removed in favour of `Density`.
