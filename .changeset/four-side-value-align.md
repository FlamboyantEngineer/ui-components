FourSidePropertyStrip gains `valueAlign` (`start`, `center`, `end`), published as `data-value-align` for the stylesheet to place each side control's value. The reference sheet aligns the side inputs' text, grows them with their value from a compact width so long, negative and decimal values show whole, and lets numbers read alike right to left.
