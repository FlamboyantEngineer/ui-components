Add relation and permission matrices and relation columns.

- `RelationMatrix` (new): row items × column items on a grid of its own (`role="grid"`) with a mark in each related cell by the relation's kind (`kinds` with `label` and `tone`), a header row and row headers pinned with sticky positions, rotated or cut column labels (`columnLabels`, cut labels shown whole in a `TruncationTooltip`), coverage counts on every row and column header (`data-uncovered` without relations), grid keyboard navigation (arrows, PageUp, PageDown, Home, End, Control+Home and Control+End, with a `data-current` crosshair on the headers), `onCellAction` to add or remove relations, and `virtualized` windowing of both rows and columns (`rowHeight`, `columnWidth`). `toggleRelation` applies a cell action to a list of relations. Hooks: `ui-relation-matrix` and its `__header`, `__corner`, `__column`, `__column-label`, `__column-count`, `__body`, `__row`, `__row-header`, `__row-label`, `__row-count`, `__cell`, `__mark` slots; `data-column-labels`, `data-virtualized`, `data-actionable`, `data-uncovered`, `data-current`, `data-related`, `data-kind`, `data-tone`.
- `PermissionMatrix` (new): roles × permissions on a `Table` with a `Checkbox` per cell, permission `groups` under collapsible group rows with a checkbox per role that grants or revokes the group, `inherited` grants drawn mixed, read-only roles (`isReadOnly`, with a lock), dirty marks against `committedValue`, and `onChange(value, change)` reporting `{ role, permissions, isGranted }`. `applyPermissionChange` applies a change. Hooks: `ui-permission-matrix` and its `__role`, `__role-label`, `__lock`, `__grant`, `__group-grant` slots; `data-readonly`, `data-inherited`, `data-dirty`.
- `RelationColumns` (new): Miller columns that walk relations upstream and downstream of an `item` through `getRelated`, each column a `ListView` with its item count and each item the count of items further along, a controlled or uncontrolled `path`, a `Breadcrumb` trail that trims the walk, `onAction` to start again from an item, and ArrowLeft and ArrowRight between columns. Hooks: `ui-relation-columns` and its `__path`, `__columns`, `__column`, `__column-header`, `__column-title`, `__column-count`, `__list`, `__item`, `__item-count`, `__empty` slots; `data-direction`, `data-depth`.
- Provider strings (new `relation-strings` module): `relationItems`, `related`, `relationCount`, `permissions`, `permissionGrant`, `permissionGroupGrant`, `readOnlyRole`, `relationItem`, `upstreamOf`, `downstreamOf`, `noUpstream`, `noDownstream`, `relatedCount` and `relationPath`.
