Add `Popover`, `Drawer` and `HoverCard`.

- `Popover` and `PopoverTrigger` (new): a dialog floating against its trigger on React Aria's `DialogTrigger`, `Popover` and `Dialog`, on the `dropdown` layer; `title`, `showCloseButton`, `showArrow`, function children with `close`, and React Aria's `placement`, `offset`, `crossOffset`, `shouldFlip`, `containerPadding`, `isNonModal`, `isKeyboardDismissDisabled`, `shouldCloseOnInteractOutside` and `triggerRef` (a custom anchor, or a popover controlled with `isOpen` and `onOpenChange` outside a trigger). Hooks: `ui-popover`, `__arrow`, `__dialog`, `__header`, `__title`, `__close`, `__body`, with React Aria's `data-placement`, `data-trigger`, `data-entering` and `data-exiting`.
- `HoverCard` and `HoverCardTrigger` (new): a card of interactive content shown on hover and keyboard focus of its trigger (React Aria `PreviewTrigger`: `delay`, `closeDelay`, `isOpen`, `defaultOpen`, `onOpenChange`, `isDisabled`), sharing the popover's placement props, arrow (`showArrow`) and layer. Hooks: `ui-hover-card`, `__arrow`, with `data-placement`, `data-trigger`, `data-entering` and `data-exiting`.
- `Drawer` and `DrawerTrigger` (new): a panel sliding in from a viewport edge on React Aria's `Sheet` family, on the `drawer` layer; `position` (`start`, `end`, `left`, `right`, `top`, `bottom`), `size` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`), `title`, `showCloseButton`, `footer`, function children with `close`, `isDismissable`, `isKeyboardDismissDisabled`, and React Aria's `preventDismissal`, `swipeDirection`, `snapPoints` and nesting. Hooks: `ui-drawer`, `__backdrop`, `__panel`, `__dialog`, `__header`, `__title`, `__close`, `__body`, `__footer`, `data-size`, and React Aria's `data-position`, `data-swipe-direction`, `data-stack-index`, `data-has-descendants`, `data-expanded`, `data-entering`, `data-exiting` and `data-sheet-content`.
- `UserCardTrigger`: with `trigger="hover"` the card opens in a `HoverCard`, so its popover also carries `ui-hover-card`; in either mode the popover now sits on the `dropdown` layer (z-index 130 by default) instead of React Aria's default z-index.
- `Modal`: an anchored dialog (`placement`) is drawn by the popover's shared panel; its hooks and behaviour are unchanged.
