Add `PdfViewer`: pages on canvas with a selectable text layer, zoom with fit modes, page navigation and find, over a PDF engine the host injects through `loadEngine` (`pdfjs-dist` 6 is an optional peer dependency). Adds the `fit-width` and `fit-page` glyphs and strings for the viewer's controls.
