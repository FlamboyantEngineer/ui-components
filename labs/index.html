Labs

Foundations

  • Icon — Every built-in glyph at every size, labelled and decorative icons, and custom icon nodes.
  • Icon Label — Leading icon and text at every size, with truncate, grow, hidden text and custom icons.
  • Provider — Built-in glyphs and strings, each shown with its default and a replacement from UiProvider.

Actions and navigation

  • Action Button — A button whose asynchronous action keeps it pending until it settles.
  • Button — Every variant, size and state of the labelled command button, with icons and content layouts.
  • Icon Button — Every variant, size and state of the icon-only command button.

Feedback and status

  • Circular Progress — A compact ring for ratios in dense cells, at three sizes.
  • Loading Overlay — A status card, the cover that blocks a loading surface and a region that makes its content inert.
  • Progress Bar — Determinate and indeterminate progress bars at three sizes, plus the decorative spinner.
  • Toast — Transient messages in four tones, drawn in place and pushed to a live region.
  • Tooltip — Hover and focus hints for controls, in two variants and every placement.
  • Truncation Tooltip — Text that shows its full content in a tooltip only while it is cut short.
