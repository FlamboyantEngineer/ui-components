# @flamboyantengineer/ui-components

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- f46b07b: Add `renderColumnActions` to `Board`: it draws the caller's controls, such as a menu or an icon button, at the end of each column header in a new `ui-board__column-actions` slot, called with the column's own entry of `columns`. A column without actions renders no slot. The controls stay in the tab order (also in a collapsed column), and pressing them never collapses the column, activates a card or starts a column drag.
- d3b1a7d: Let a host run the full `Chat` with its own controls and asynchronous sending:
  
  - **Quote in assistant chats:** `onReply` now offers Reply (which a host can name Quote through the provider strings) on finished messages with text in assistant chats.
  - **Composer controls:** `composerToolbar` places the host's controls before the model picker, and the composer's tools wrap in a narrow composer. `Chat` forwards `allowsEmptySubmit`, `submitShortcut`, `minRows` and `maxRows`.
  - **Asynchronous sending:** `onSend`, `onReply` and `onEdit` may return a promise. The composer keeps the message, its tokens and the quote until the host accepts it, sends it only once meanwhile, keeps them for another try when it rejects, and reports the error through `onSendError`. The message settles in the conversation it was sent from.
  - **Composers:** `MessageComposer` and `RichMessageComposer` accept a promise from `onSubmit`, publish `data-submitting` while it is pending, and report failures through `onSubmitError`.
- ce92334: Add message display options. `ChatMessage` takes `showAvatar`, `showAuthorName`, `showTime` and `variant` (`bubble`, the default, or `plain`), published as `data-variant`, with `data-avatar-hidden` while the avatar is hidden. `Chat` takes `showAvatars`, `showAuthorNames`, `showTimes`, `sentVariant` and `receivedVariant`, so a conversation can drop avatars, names and times and draw replies as plain text on the page while the reader's own messages keep their bubble. Each message keeps its author and time in its accessible name. The defaults keep today's look.
- 412dfac: A model and reasoning-effort picker for chat composers.
  
  - `ChatModelPicker` (from the package root and `@flamboyantengineer/ui-components/chat-model-picker`) shows the current model, and its effort when the model takes one, on a compact button that opens a menu: the models with their descriptions, badges and the reasons unavailable ones cannot be chosen, then the efforts the chosen model takes. The model and the effort are each controlled or not (`model`, `defaultModel` and `onModelChange`; `effort`, `defaultEffort` and `onEffortChange`); choosing a model that does not take the current effort moves it to the model's default effort and reports it, and `persistKey` keeps the choice in the provider storage.
  - `Chat` takes `modelPicker` and shows the picker among its composer's tools, beside the attach and emoji buttons, disabled with the composer.
  - Styling hooks (`ui-chat-model-picker`, its `model`, `effort` and `menu` slots and `data-has-effort`), provider strings (`chatModel`, `chatEffort`, `chatModelTrigger`), a lab and a reference stylesheet.
- f1c2726: Question cards accept answers asynchronously.
  
  - `ChatQuestion`'s `onSubmit` and `onDismiss` may return a promise. While it is unsettled the card publishes `data-submitting`, the pressed button is pending and keeps focus, the answers are read-only and a second press sends nothing. A rejection or a throw keeps every chosen option, typed answer and the question shown, and announces why in `ui-chat-question__error` (the error's message, else the new `questionSubmitFailed` string, or the common `actionFailed` for a dismissal), so the reader can retry. The card keeps the answers until the host changes its `status`.
  - Without `onSubmit` a pending card is read-only: no Submit, options and fields that take no changes (`data-readonly` on the options), and Next to step through its questions.
  - A card's drafts, step and requests belong to its `id`: a card that changes `id` starts afresh, and a request settling after the card changed, settled or unmounted changes nothing.
  - `ChatParts`' and `Chat`'s `onAnswerQuestion` and `onDismissQuestion` may return a promise the card awaits, and `Chat` starts every message's parts afresh when `conversationId` changes, so a question's drafts and pending request never carry over to another conversation.
- 02682eb: Show a pending `ChatQuestion`'s questions as tabs, with a counter ("2/3") at the end of their row. Every tab is clickable at any time, focus stays on the tab, the arrow keys move between tabs, and the tabs scroll with buttons when they do not fit. Back, Next, Submit and Dismiss work as before. A card with one question shows one tab and "1/1".
  
  An answered question's tab is described as Answered, and the new `answeredMark` prop (`icon`, the default, or `style`) chooses whether it also draws a check or leaves `data-answered` to the stylesheet. `ChatParts` takes it as `questionAnsweredMark`, and `Chat` forwards it.
  
  Hooks: `ui-chat-question__tabs`, `__tab` and `__panel` replace `__steps`, `__step` and `__header`. `data-current` goes away (the tab's `data-selected` takes its place), and the root gains `data-answered-mark`. The provider gains the `questionCounter` and `questionAnsweredTab` strings.
- ca75509: Show what an assistant is thinking or doing inside its reply, in the styles common assistant chats use:
  
  - **Thinking in the reply:** while a reply streams and the assistant thinks or acts, `Chat` ends the reply with a `ChatStatusLine` (`ui-chat__status`) instead of a line below the transcript. It reads the reply's `statusText`, else the new `thinkingText` prop, else "Thinking…", and `thinkingIcon` replaces its busy indicator.
  - **How long it shows:** `thinkingUntil` keeps the line until the reply's first `content`, until its `text` (the default: through reasoning and tool calls), or until its `end`, showing the indicator or icon alone while the text streams in.
  - **`ChatStatusLine`:** `text` is optional and defaults to the provider string `chatThinking` ("Thinking…"); `icon` shows in place of the busy indicator (`ui-chat-status-line__icon`); `activity` `writing` shows the indicator alone and publishes `data-activity`.
  - **Reasoning summaries:** a reasoning part's `title` labels it while it streams, and labels its running group of steps. `ChatReasoning` and `ChatSteps` take `title` and `expandsAutomatically`; `ChatParts` and `Chat` take `expandsActivity`, which keeps reasoning and steps closed while they run.
  - **Reference stylesheet:** the status line's text and the labels of streaming reasoning and running steps shimmer; the status line's icon pulses. Both hold still under reduced motion.
  - **Typing indicator:** `ChatTypingIndicator` only says who is typing; its `variant` prop, `ChatTypingIndicatorVariant` and `data-variant` are removed, and `chatThinking` is now a plain string.
- b0a431c: Chat components for assistant conversations and conversations between people.
  
  - `Chat` (from `@flamboyantengineer/ui-components/chat`) composes a whole conversation from the host's messages, participants and callbacks: grouped messages with parts, delivery, edits, deletions, reactions, read receipts, unread and older history; Copy, ratings, Reply, React, Edit and Delete; typing and status lines; a queue of follow-ups; header search; and a rich composer with mentions, commands, attachments, drafts and Stop. `ChatHeader` shows the participants, their presence, the title and a status line.
  - `useChatController` turns a host's `send` function, an async iterable of `ChatStreamEvent`s, into a chat's state and props: streaming, Stop, Retry, a send queue, approvals and questions awaited mid-stream, Always allow, recall history and uploads with progress. `reduceChatStream` and `isAwaitingReader` expose its model.
  - `ChatParts` renders a message's typed parts (text as Markdown or plain with mention and command pills, reasoning, tool calls, questions, plans, sources, attachments, code and diffs) and folds reasoning and tool calls into step groups, with `toolRenderers`, `renderPart` and kept expansion state.
  - `ChatTranscript` is a keyboard-navigable feed with batched announcements, follow and anchor scrolling, older history, highlighting, an unread bar, read tracking and virtualization. `ChatMessage` shows its author with presence, grouping, delivery, errors with Retry, quotes, edits, deletions, reactions and readers; `ChatQuote`, `ChatDateSeparator`, `ChatUnreadDivider`, `ChatTypingIndicator`, `ChatReadReceipts` and `groupChatMessages` complete it.
  - `MessageComposer` offers a banner, attachments by button, drop and paste, an emoji picker, edit mode, recall, a persisted draft and sending while a reply is pending. `RichMessageComposer` (from `@flamboyantengineer/ui-components/message-composer/rich`) adds mention and command pills, typed Markdown and code blocks, and sends `{ text, tokens }`.
  - `ChatToolCall` shows a tool call's status, arguments and result in a collapsible card, with Approve, Deny with a reason and Always allow.
  - `ChatQuestion` asks one or several questions answered by choice, with recommendations, previews and Other, or in free text, collapsing to a summary.
  - `ChatReasoning`, `ChatSteps`, `ChatPlan` and `ChatStatusLine` show an assistant's reasoning, grouped steps, plan and current activity, announcing plan and status changes politely.
  - `ChatAttachment` and `ChatAttachmentList` show thumbnails that open the media viewer, file chips, upload progress, errors with Retry, downloads and removal that keeps focus in the list.
  - `ChatSources` and `ChatCitation` list an answer's sources and preview them from inline numbered citations; `createCitationOverride` adds citations to Markdown.
  - `ChatMessageActions` and `ChatMessageActionsMenu` offer a message's commands as a toolbar and a context menu sharing one state, with feedback details, an emoji picker and a delete confirmation.
  - `ChatList` lists conversations with search, New chat, selection, presence, unread counts, muted state, host actions, loading and empty states.
  - `CodeBlock` shows code with its language, Copy, an optional Apply action, highlighting and a terminal variant; `createCodeBlockOverride` draws Markdown code blocks with it.
  - `Markdown` renders streaming sources with `isStreaming`, completing unfinished syntax and re-rendering only the blocks that changed.
  - Styling hooks, provider strings, glyphs, labs and reference stylesheets for each of them.
- 4f0e10d: Let a host's own controls insert tokens into a composer's draft. `RichMessageComposer` takes `handleRef`, which receives `insertToken` (a pill at the caret, or where the caret was before focus left, else at the end, as one undo step, then focus) and `getDraft`; insertion reports `inserted`, or refuses with `disabled`, `editing`, `submitting` or `unavailable`. `Chat` takes `composerHandleRef`, a handle bound to the shown conversation whose `insertToken` also refuses with `other-conversation`, and `onDraftChange`, which reports the conversation's draft as it loads and after each edit so the host can skip tokens already in it.
- b082f85: A "+" menu for the message composers:
  
  - `MessageComposer` and `RichMessageComposer` take `menu` (`{ sections, filterable? }`): a "+" button at the start of the toolbar, in place of the attach button, opens a menu above the composer with a heading per section. Items (`MessageComposerMenuItem`) have an icon, a label, a note, a shortcut hint, a disabled state with a reason, a toggle state the host controls (`isSelected` with `onSelectedChange`, which keeps the menu open) and submenus. An item's `onAction` receives the composer's helpers (`MessageComposerHelpers`): `insertText` and `insertPill` at the caret (a pill in the rich composer, its text in the plain one), `attachFiles` (the system file chooser, then `onAttach`) and `focusInput`. `filterable` leads the menu with a search field that narrows the items by label and note. Drop and paste of files keep working.
  - `Chat` forwards `composerMenu`; its tools are the "+" button, the emoji button, `composerToolbar`, the model picker, then Send or Stop.
  - The plain entry point now also exports `MessageComposerToken` and `MessageComposerTokenKind`.
  - Styling hooks `ui-message-composer__menu-button` and `ui-message-composer__menu`, the provider string `composerMenu`, labs and reference styles.
- 2c3282c: Unify choice groups, list/icon/tree selects, and code fields with embedded editing surfaces. Add number bases and unit examples, calendar highlights and availability states, explicit tri-state boolean displays, preset and custom code syntax, and diagnostic marks inside custom scrollbar tracks. Improve sticky-header demonstrations and align list selection controls and drag handles beside their content.
- c26c480: Releases are git tags in the repository instead of a registry package. Each `v<version>` tag holds the built package, so a host installs an exact version with `npm install "git+https://github.com/FlamboyantEngineer/ui-components.git#v<version>"` and a token that can read the repository.
- b606ce0: Add opt-in sequential entry animation to every SegmentedBar shape, with first-visibility triggering, proportional timing, reduced-motion support and replay examples. Keep circular indeterminate progress centered and preserve popover spacing when scroll locking reserves scrollbar space.
- c3ad056: Initial release of the unstyled, accessible React component library.
  
  - Components for actions, fields, navigation, overlays, layout, collections, editors and data display, built on React Aria where it provides the behaviour.
  - Documented styling hooks and shared providers for glyphs, strings, locale, links and persisted state.
  - Static component labs with variant, size and state examples, optional reference stylesheets and accessibility coverage.
  - Testing helpers for driving components through their accessible roles and labels.
- b082f85: Menu items can be check items of their own: `MenuItem` (and a menu item entry) takes `isSelected` in a menu or section without selection, and draws a `menuitemcheckbox` with `aria-checked`, `data-selected` and the check slot among plain items, such as a toggle in a menu of actions. Activating it calls `onAction`, for the caller to flip the state; `shouldCloseOnSelect={false}` keeps the menu open.
- b3ad6ef: Add inline record references to the rich composer and Chat. `MessageComposerToken` and `ChatTextToken` take the kind `reference`, with the record's opaque `id`, the `label` the reference shows and an optional host `recordType`. `RichMessageComposer`, `ChatParts` and `Chat` take `getReference`, a `RecordLabelResolver` that returns the record's current `RecordLabelPresentation` (name, icon, link, kind, version, state, preview, or missing or restricted) by its id; references draw as atomic `RecordLabel` chips in the editor (with a remove button) and in messages, in the new `reference` value of `data-kind` on `ui-message-composer__pill` and `ui-chat-parts__token`. The menu's `insertPill` inserts references, and references keep their identity through sending, a refused send, editing, quoting and the stored draft. `RecordLabelReference`, `RecordLabelPresentation` and `RecordLabelResolver` are exported.

### Patch Changes

- bdfc96a: Group every Chat lab under Conversations with a first section that shows every Chat feature in one conversation. In the reference stylesheet, give the message composer a white fill while enabled and a grey one while disabled, and keep the chat list's header inside a narrow list.
- d28be49: `ChatParts` keeps a failed tool call discoverable inside its group of steps.
  
  - A group holding a failed call stays open once the run ends and the answer follows, while a group of successful steps still collapses.
  - When a call newly fails, including in the update that ends the run, the group and the card the reader had closed open again, as if the reader had opened them; `onExpandedPartsChange` receives them, so `Chat` and other hosts that keep `expandedParts` see the change. Closing them again holds until another call newly fails.
  - A call that had already failed when the parts mount, as in loaded history or a remount under virtualization, leaves a kept closed choice alone.
- f4646ac: Draw every field frame in the reference stylesheet from shared field tokens: the same fill, border and thin focus ring in fields, search boxes, pickers, editors and message composers, a grey fill with muted text when disabled (instead of fading the whole field), and a grey fill with a dashed border when read-only.
- 3183e27: Menus opened from a `MenuTrigger` (the composer "+" menu, the model picker and other `MenuPopover` menus) no longer lock the page: it stays scrollable, reachable and visible to assistive technology while the menu is open. As the page scrolls, the menu stays open and keeps its place against its trigger, a sticky or fixed one included; scrolling an element that holds the trigger still closes it. `DropdownMenu` menus no longer close when the page scrolls. `ContextMenu` still holds the page still, like a platform context menu.
